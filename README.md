# 📞 AstroSage Call Center Performance Analysis (Microsoft Excel)

> An end-to-end Excel dashboard project focused on analyzing call center operations, customer satisfaction, agent performance, and business profitability to recommend the optimal allocation of a ₹1 Crore investment.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

AstroSage, a leading astrology consultation platform, received an investment of **₹1 Crore** to improve its call center operations. The objective of this project was to analyze historical call center data and recommend how this investment should be allocated to maximize operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and profitability.

Using Microsoft Excel, I cleaned the raw dataset, built an interactive dashboard, analyzed key performance indicators (KPIs), and generated business recommendations based on data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The management wanted answers to questions such as:

- Should the investment be used to hire more agents?
- Should employee training be improved?
- Should call center technology be upgraded?
- Which factors affect customer satisfaction?
- How can operational efficiency be improved?
- What drives revenue and repeat customers?

This project provides analytical answers to support strategic decision-making.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Daily call volume fluctuates significantly, indicating the need for better workforce planning.
- Customer satisfaction improved slightly over time but remains moderate.
- Call consultations generate the majority of total revenue.
- A large proportion of users provide lower ratings, highlighting opportunities for service improvement.
- Repeat callers contribute significantly to overall business, indicating strong customer retention.
- Higher customer satisfaction is associated with improved business performance.

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I recommend:

- Upgrade call center technology to improve operational efficiency.
- Invest in agent training to improve customer satisfaction.
- Schedule additional staffing during peak demand periods.
- Reward high-performing gurus based on customer ratings.
- Introduce loyalty programs for repeat customers.
- Continuously monitor KPIs using an interactive dashboard.
